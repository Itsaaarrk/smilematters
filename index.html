About Smile Matters

Because your smile matters.

Smile Matters Dental Care Center started in Pasig and now cares for patients in three clinics: Pasig, SMILE 2.0 in Marikina, and Smile Matters Dental Studio in Caloocan.

Modern and affordable dental care

We are dedicated to providing modern and affordable dental care. From routine cleanings to cosmetic makeovers, our experienced team is here to help you smile with confidence.

Patients tell us what they value most: dentists who set expectations and explain every step, a clean and hygienic clinic, and flexible payment options.

  • Modern, affordable care with flexible payment options and HMO for selected procedures.
  • Dentists who explain first, so every procedure feels calm and clear.
  • One account, three branches, with online booking and a patient portal.
